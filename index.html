For venue managers

Keep your roster and bookings organised.

Bring your artists, schedules, booking details, and invoices together across your venues.

  • Build your roster and invite artists.
  • Plan recurring schedules and individual gigs.
  • Send booking requests with the agreed fee.
  • Share booking notes with artists.
  • Track gig costs and follow up on invoices.
For artists & DJs

Keep your gigs and invoicing organised.

Manage venue bookings and private gigs, keep your availability current, and organise your invoices.

  • Accept or decline booking requests.
  • Keep venue and private gigs on one calendar.
  • Manage your availability.
  • Track fees for completed and upcoming gigs.
  • Create an invoice or upload your own.
