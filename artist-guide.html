Artist quick start guide

Welcome to Nexgig

Nexgig brings venue managers and performing artists together around one clear booking workflow.

Keep track of bookings you receive from venues already on the platform and bookings you add yourself, all in one place.

Built on trust

We check that venues are real and that the people managing them are authorised to do so.

Your booking details, including fees and invoices, are private. Nexgig and your venue are committed under a signed agreement to protecting this information. Access is limited, and database access through our verified administrative tools is logged.

Getting started

Your venue invites you to Nexgig. Sign up, then use these screens to manage your bookings:

  • Home & Notifications: see upcoming bookings, respond to requests and get updates.
  • Schedule & Calendar: organise bookings and availability.
  • Billing: create and send invoices.
  • Profile: manage your details and ask for help.

Home, requests & updates

Your bookings, requests and updates in one place.

Home

  • The week strip shows your weekly schedule. Tap a day to see its bookings.
  • Next up lists your upcoming bookings in date order.
  • Gig Log keeps your booking history, including venue bookings and bookings you add yourself.

Needs Attention

  • Needs Attention shows new booking requests and bookings cancelled by venues.
  • Tap a request to view its details.

Notifications

  • Turn on notifications for booking requests, booking changes and invoice reminders.
  • Tap Update whenever it appears to get the latest improvements. We’re always improving the design and adding features.

Schedule & calendar

Keep your bookings and availability in one place.

Schedule

  • This is your monthly calendar.
  • Tap + to add your own bookings, including private events or bookings at venues outside Nexgig.
  • Shown as unavailable: This records the booking and shows managers when you’re unavailable. Your private booking details stay private.

Connect your calendar

  • Phone Calendar works one way: Send your bookings from Nexgig to your phone’s calendar.
  • Google Calendar works two ways: Send your bookings from Nexgig to Google Calendar and sync your Google busy times into Nexgig so venues know your availability.
  • Venue managers see only unavailable times imported from your calendar, never the details of your commitments.

Billing & invoices

Know when to invoice, then choose how to send it.

Billing

  • Each venue has its own billing cycle. When it ends, you’ll see how many bookings are ready to invoice. Tap the venue to create an invoice.

Create invoice

  • Tap Preview invoice to generate an invoice in Nexgig and review it.
  • Or choose Upload my own to use your own tax invoice.

Artist profile & help

Keep your details current. Tell us how we can help.

Artist Profile

Your profile

  • Keep your artist details, bio and links up to date.
  • Recently played shows the last five venues you played at.
  • Tap the Network button to see the venues you’re connected to.

Need help?

Tap the floating message button on your Artist Profile to send questions, report a problem or suggest a feature.

We’re glad you’re here. Here’s to new opportunities and great connections.
