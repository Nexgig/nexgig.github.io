Common questions

How do I get started as a venue manager?

Request access on the Nexgig website and we’ll review your request and help you get started. Manager accounts require approval from the Nexgig team.

How do I book an artist?

Open the calendar, add a set, pick a date and time, then assign an artist from your venue’s lineup and send the request.

How do I receive bookings as an artist?

A venue manager can add you to their roster and send you booking requests through Nexgig. If you’re invited by email before joining, follow the invitation to get started. You can accept or decline requests in the app and manage your own private gigs too.

How do I delete my account?

Go to Settings and choose Delete Account. This anonymizes your data and cancels active bookings. You can also email us to request deletion.

Who can use Nexgig?

Nexgig is for venue managers and performing artists aged 18 and over. Both use the same app.
